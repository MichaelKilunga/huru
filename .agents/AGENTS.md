# Project Rules - HuruLearn

## User Interface & Aesthetics
- **No AI Branding/Mentions**: Do not use the word "AI", "AI-powered", "Artificial Intelligence", "Bot", or "AI Assistant" in user-facing texts, page titles, or interface labels. Present HuruLearn strictly as a direct, instant legal and educational guidance service.
- **Governmental Color Palette**: Use official Tanzanian public system colors: solid Forest Green (`#166534` / `#15803d`), Deep Royal Blue (`#1e40af` / `#1d4ed8`), and crisp white/off-white (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`). Do not use brown or amber as public system colors.
- **Bilingual & Human-Friendly Themes**: Default to clean, welcoming, and high-readability light backgrounds (e.g. white, off-white, soft slate) for public, school, and student-facing interfaces. Avoid dark/neon-heavy themes unless explicitly requested.
- **Solid Colors Over Gradients**: Do not use gradient backgrounds, glow effects, or AI-generated decorative icons. Use clean, solid, grounded colors matching authentic Tanzanian governmental and public educational interfaces.
- **SMS Capability Preservation**: Do not remove SMS shortcode details (such as the 15054 shortcode and instructions) when refactoring layouts. Ensure SMS-based learning instructions remain clearly visible as a primary offline entry channel.
- **SMS Keyword Visibility**: The SMS keyword (`HURU`) and shortcode (`15054`) must always be clearly displayed on the homepage and on signup/login screens as a primary offline access method.
