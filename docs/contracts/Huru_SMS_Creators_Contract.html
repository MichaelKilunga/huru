SCHEDULE – Description of the Work and the Contribution

Title of the Work: Huru SMS

Full description: Huru SMS – Multichannel SMS and Web Citizen Information and Guidance System for Tanzania, version 1.0. A multichannel digital service through which any person in Tanzania asks a question in Kiswahili or English by SMS (the keyword HURU followed by the question, sent to shortcode 15054) or through a web chat, and receives a short, practical answer grounded in Tanzanian law, institutions, places and verified knowledge, with referral to nearby services and official contacts. The Work comprises the server application (PHP / Laravel), its database design, the web chat and public website, the administration panel, the written content in both languages, the visual designs, and the accompanying documentation.

Owner and applicant: HURU DIGITAL CO. LTD.

Version: 1.0     Year of completion: 2026     Country of origin: United Republic of Tanzania
